02 / Study design

An interpretable experiment.
Not a black box.

One molecular structure enters the pipeline; three molecular parameters emerge. We examine whether their errors are acceptable after they pass through the EOS.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can simple structure-derived descriptors reproduce pure-component vapor–liquid equilibrium through learned PC-SAFT parameters?

A molecule is represented by its SMILES structure. Its published fitted PC-SAFT parameters are supervised labels, and FeOs calculates saturation properties from those predicted parameters.

Prediction-time information: molecular structure only, not measured saturation pressures, liquid densities or reference parameters.

05 / Scientific findings

Evidence before
easy conclusions.

The purpose is to understand what worked, where it failed, and why physical properties behave differently from ML parameter errors.

01

A simpler model can give better vapor pressure.

Ridge + 5 achieved 16.22% development pressure APE versus 25.91% for Extra Trees + 156. Parameter-level improvements did not translate directly to lower EOS pressure error.

02

Density and pressure reward different parameter combinations.

Ridge + 12 reached 1.79% development liquid-density error, while Ridge + 5 gave lower pressure error. A single “best model” depends on the property of interest.

03

The nonlinear learner shows a training–validation gap.

Extra Trees fits training targets closely but its validation errors rise substantially. It still beats Ridge on individual parameter MAEs on that split. The VLE comparison remains a separate test.

LOCAL PRESSURE SENSITIVITY

Small parameter changes, large pressure effects.

Five development molecules were perturbed ±1% in each PC-SAFT parameter. The resulting derivatives suggest sensitivity to dispersion energy and potential cancellation or reinforcement among parameter errors. These are local approximations, not causal proof.

Sensitivity measurements become available after the export.

06 / Reproducibility

Every conclusion
needs a trail.

Source data, preprocessing, model selection and the one-time final held-out evaluation should be inspectable, not just asserted.

01

Data and filtering

Esper2023 parameters from the FeOs repository. Retain saturated, nonassociating C/H-only molecules with 3–20 carbon atoms. Canonicalize SMILES and remove duplicate structures.

Explore source collection ↗
02

Preprocessing

Generate descriptors using RDKit. Apply median imputation and scaling inside a scikit-learn pipeline fitted exclusively on the appropriate training data.

Features: molecular weight, carbon count, rings, rotatable bonds, branch points.

03

Model selection

Evaluate mean baseline, Ridge, Random Forest and Extra Trees. Compare parameter errors using three-fold training-only CV and VLE performance using a separate development split.

Frozen choice: Ridge(alpha=1.0), 5 descriptors.

04

Final evaluation

Refit selected Ridge on the 155 original training molecules. Evaluate exactly once on 39 held-out molecules at four reference-defined temperatures each. Report all failures and extreme errors.

156/156 reference and ML state comparisons converged.

What this project does not claim

No experimental property accuracy is claimed from the reference-PC-SAFT comparisons. No mixtures, polar/association terms, live browser inference or end-to-end differentiable thermodynamic training were used.

Reproduction notes

Static results are exported from saved CSVs. The repository should include the data-download process, requirements, scripts, immutable test split, evaluation configuration and AI-assistance disclosure.
